How Buyer Inquiries Work

Straightforward bulk trade, handled with commercial clarity.

The goal is not to rush into a vague quote. The goal is to understand the requirement properly enough to say whether current supply and movement context fit the need.

  • share grade, quantity, packing format, destination, and timing
  • review the requirement against current sourcing context
  • keep the discussion grounded in workable commercial terms
  • move toward dispatch planning only when the fit is real

Before you submit

A faster quote starts with a tighter brief.

  • say whether the need is repeat or one-time
  • mention if you are flexible on grade or packing
  • include destination city and timing expectation
  • note any quality, appearance, or retail presentation concerns

What We Need To Quote Properly

The best buying conversations begin with a few practical basics.

Grade size clarity

Start with the grade you actually need

Whether the requirement is 4, 5, 6, or 7 suta, the quote works better when the grade is stated upfront.

Quantity volume band

Real numbers make the discussion faster

Quantity band matters because a 100 kg inquiry and a multi-tonne inquiry do not move in the same way.

Packing format

Packing format changes the commercial fit

Loose bulk, 10 kg bags, 25 kg bags, and retail/private label discussion each imply a different buying context.

Destination movement

Destination and timing are part of the quote

The requirement becomes more useful when the buyer can say where it needs to go and how quickly it has to move.
