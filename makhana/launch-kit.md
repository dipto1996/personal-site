# Makhanamart Launch Kit

Practical copy and outreach notes for the first launch of the Makhanamart site.

## Positioning

Makhanamart is a Malda-based makhana trading company working across the eastern
crop belt. The business buys and sells makhana in bulk, with procurement routes
through Purnia, Harishchandrapur, and Raiganj.

Use this framing consistently:

- origin-led sourcing
- grade-wise bulk trade
- practical buying and selling desk
- Malda base with eastern belt access

Avoid this framing:

- superfood hype as the primary identity
- vague “platform” language
- export-heavy positioning before domestic trade flow is proven

## Buyer Outreach Flow

### Who to contact first

- wholesalers and distributors in Kolkata and Siliguri
- snack brands looking for repeat bulk supply
- exporters and merchant traders who already understand the category
- regional packaged-food businesses that may want plain or packed makhana

### Minimum information to ask from a buyer

- grade needed
- quantity band
- delivery city
- packaging format
- timing of requirement
- whether the requirement is repeat or one-time

### First outreach email

Subject:

`Bulk makhana supply from Malda procurement belt`

Body:

`Hello {Name},`

`I’m reaching out from Makhanamart, a makhana trading company based in Malda, West Bengal. We source grade-wise lots through Purnia, Harishchandrapur, and Raiganj and are currently building relationships with bulk buyers for 4, 5, 6, and 7 suta supply.`

`If you are buying makhana in bulk, I can share available grades, quantity bands, and dispatch details based on your requirement.`

`Please let me know your current buying grade, quantity, and destination city.`

`Regards,`

`Makhanamart`

`Hello, this is Makhanamart from Malda. We deal in bulk makhana and source through Purnia, Harishchandrapur, and Raiganj. If you are currently buying 4, 5, 6, or 7 suta lots, share your grade, quantity, and destination and I can revert with availability.`

## Seller Intake Checklist

Before taking a seller lead seriously, ask for:

- location
- available quantity
- grade or stock type
- current lot condition
- expected price
- readiness for pickup or dispatch

## First 30 Days

- use the site to collect buyer and seller leads in one place
- qualify every inquiry by grade, quantity, and geography
- prioritize repeat bulk buyers over casual retail inquiries
- build a small but credible rhythm of quote, sample, and dispatch conversations
- keep records of who asked for which grade and which region they buy for
