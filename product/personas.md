# Personas

## Founder / Owner

Wants revenue growth without building a large sourcing, bid, and export operations team from day one.

## Procurement / Sourcing Lead

Needs to verify whether a supplier is real, capable, and worth engaging before wasting time in outreach or qualification calls.

## BD Lead

Needs a repeatable way to spot tenders by category, geography, and buyer type without combing through multiple portals manually.

## Bid Manager

Needs quick decision support on fit, gaps, urgency, and whether a bid is even worth preparing.

## Export Manager

Needs a market-by-market view of readiness, documentation gaps, and next actions before pursuing overseas buyers.
