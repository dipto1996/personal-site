# User Problem Notes

- Supplier buyers do not need another directory. They need cross-checked trust before outreach.
- Tender users do not need all tenders. They need the small subset they can actually win.
- Exporters do not need generic export advice. They need route-specific readiness and blocker visibility.
- Tender and exporter workflows are document-heavy, fragmented, and operationally slow.
- Trust matters. If the product feels like noisy scraping or unsupported scoring, it will not convert.
