# Release Scope

## In scope

- Public product microsurface inside the portfolio site
- Product-suite landing page with a live shared company graph
- Demo-quality but real supplier, tender, and export workflows
- Shared workspace auth, invites, and persisted product state
- Workspace control center for alerts, billing, runtime status, and source sync
- Transparent scoring and source-backed reasoning
- Persistent shortlist state in-browser
- Product narrative and CTA
- Environment-backed Postgres, Resend, Stripe, and cron support with local fallbacks
- Same-origin mutation protection and rate limiting on write routes

## Out of scope

- Full sanctioned Udyam company-level access
- Full MCA company-level master-data automation
- Broad public rollout before environment provisioning
