# Competitor Matrix

## VerifySME benchmarks

| Product | Who it serves | Core promise | Strong points | Weak points / gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tofler | Finance, diligence, and company research teams | Company intelligence and MCA-backed business data | Clear company-360 model, comparators, risk analysis, public pricing | Diligence-first, not sourcing-first |
| IndiaMART | Buyers and sellers across Indian categories | B2B discovery and inquiry routing | Huge supply coverage and search habit | Trust burden stays with the buyer |
| TradeIndia | Indian B2B buyers and sellers | Listings, discovery, and lead routing | Explicit trust messaging and fraud-awareness cues | Directory UX and lead-first experience |
| ExportersIndia | Export-oriented buyers and sellers | Manufacturer discovery and lead generation | Buyer/seller split and export orientation | Weak verification depth |

## TenderRadar benchmarks

| Product | Who it serves | Core promise | Strong points | Weak points / gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BidAssist | Indian contractors and suppliers | Find and track tenders quickly | Strong alerting pattern, transparent self-serve pricing, familiar search UX | Search-first and still broad for SMEs |
| TenderTiger ATOMS | Bid teams and enterprise operators | Tender-to-bid workflow automation | PQ extraction, annexure workflow, compliance framing | Heavy enterprise posture |
| Tender247 | Indian bid teams | Discovery plus services and analytics | Broader workflow ambition | Dense value communication and sales-led packaging |
| TenderDetail | Tender-search buyers | Alert-led discovery and archives | Subscription clarity | Legacy interaction model and weaker fit logic |

## ExportPulse benchmarks

| Product | Who it serves | Core promise | Strong points | Weak points / gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DGFT Trade Connect | Indian exporters | Official export guidance and workflows | Task-based IA, official trust, country/buyer guidance | Credential/OTP friction before value |
| Volza | Import-export researchers and sales teams | Trade-data intelligence and company / HS search | Strong product/company/HS-code search and transparent plans | Data-heavy, not readiness-heavy |
| Exim Trade Data | Traders and exporters | Import-export records and buyer discovery | Transparent pricing and importer/exporter discovery | Workflow-light |
| ExportersIndia | Export discovery users | Lead generation and buyer discovery | Category breadth and exporter orientation | Directory-first, not market-readiness-first |

## Patterns to adapt

- `Tofler`: company-360 and comparator framing
- `BidAssist`: free-search to paid-workflow motion
- `TenderTiger`: qualification and compliance workflow depth
- `Trade Connect`: task-based export IA
- `Volza`: company / product / HS-code search patterns

## Patterns to avoid

- SEO-heavy link farms before core product value
- Consultancy-heavy funnels before self-serve proof
- Opaque pricing and gated value discovery
- Raw portal UX with no operational interpretation layer

## White-space opportunity

- `VerifySME`: discovery plus trust in one flow
- `TenderRadar`: company-aware tender fit instead of generic alerting
- `ExportPulse`: route readiness and blocker sequencing instead of raw trade data
