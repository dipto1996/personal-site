# Product Requirements Document

## Product

TradeGraph India

## One-line thesis

TradeGraph India helps Indian SMEs verify suppliers, qualify tenders, and assess export readiness on top of one shared company graph.

## Problem

Indian SME workflows are fragmented across registries, portals, PDFs, and manual research. Teams lose time on three linked jobs:

- deciding whether a supplier is trustworthy
- deciding whether a tender is actually worth bidding
- deciding whether a company is ready to export into a specific market

## Target users

- SME founder or operator
- Procurement or sourcing lead
- Bid manager
- Export manager / commercial lead

## Buyer

Founder, commercial head, procurement lead, or export lead at an Indian SME.

## Launch wedge

A public-facing interactive product pilot embedded in the personal site:

- `VerifySME` on [`solutions.html`](../solutions.html)
- `TenderRadar` on [`dashboards.html`](../dashboards.html)
- `ExportPulse` on [`solutions.html`](../solutions.html)

## Must-have outcomes

- User immediately understands the shared company-graph thesis
- User can verify suppliers using source-backed signals
- User can inspect tender fit, urgency, and qualification gaps
- User can assess export route readiness and blockers
- User can save shortlist state locally and see persistent workflow behavior

## Must-have features

- VerifySME supplier filters, presets, shortlist, compare, and dossier view
- TenderRadar profile presets, fit scoring, search, shortlist, and tender detail
- ExportPulse exporter presets, route scoring, blocker view, shortlist, and next actions
- Clear navigation and positioning across the portfolio site
- Browser-side analytics hooks and persistence

## Nice-to-have later

- Live source adapters
- Team workspaces
- Alerts and notifications
- CRM / CSV exports beyond VerifySME shortlist
- Auth and billing

## Success metric

Qualified product-interest from Indian SMEs who see credible workflow value and request follow-up.
