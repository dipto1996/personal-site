What helps us quote properly.

A tighter brief produces a tighter commercial response. If the need is serious, these are the details that help us assess fit without unnecessary back-and-forth.

  • grade needed: 4, 5, 6, 7 suta or open to discussion
  • quantity band and whether the requirement is repeat or one-time
  • packing need: loose bulk, 10 kg, 25 kg, or retail/private label
  • destination city and expected dispatch timing
