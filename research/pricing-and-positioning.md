# Pricing And Positioning

## Positioning hypothesis

TradeGraph India is not three random tools. It is an operating system for supplier trust, tender qualification, and export readiness for Indian SMEs.

## Packaging hypothesis

- Free public view: interactive pilot with preset profiles and shortlist behavior
- Starter: saved profiles, alerts, and CSV exports
- Growth: team seats, watchlists, and history / source freshness
- Custom: category monitoring, procurement intelligence, and export route advisory

## Commercial message

Pay to stop wasting time on bad suppliers, bad bids, and bad export routes.
